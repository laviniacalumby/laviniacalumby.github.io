Program Delivery

All coaching services are delivered online. Your personalized training program, wellness guidance, and educational materials are delivered digitally via email or client portal. Lavinia works with clients worldwide and across all time zones.

Check-In Calls

Monthly check-in calls are included in all program tiers and are conducted via Zoom or FaceTime at a mutually agreed time. Calls are typically 30 minutes. Please be on time and in a quiet space. To reschedule, at least 24 hours notice is required.

Communication

All ongoing communication takes place via email or direct message. Lavinia aims to respond within 24 hours on business days. WhatsApp or Instagram DM may be used for brief check-ins depending on the agreed format at the start of your program.

Payment

Programs are billed monthly. Payment is due in full at the beginning of each month before program materials are delivered. Payment is accepted via Zelle or Venmo. By enrolling you authorize recurring monthly payments for the duration of your program. No refunds are issued for partially used months.

Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your program at any time by providing written notice to lavinia@laviniacalumby.com at least 7 days before your next billing date. Cancellations received after this window will take effect the following month. No partial refunds are issued.

Health & Medical

The client confirms that all information provided in the PAR-Q and intake form is accurate and complete. If your health status changes during the program — including pregnancy, injury, illness, or new diagnosis — please inform Lavinia immediately before continuing training. Lavinia is a certified personal trainer and holistic health coach and her services do not replace the advice of a licensed medical professional.

Confidentiality

All personal information, health data, and communications shared with Lavinia are kept strictly confidential and will never be shared with third parties without your explicit written consent.

Photography & Testimonials

Lavinia will never share photos, progress images, or identifying information about clients without explicit written consent. Any testimonials shared publicly will be approved by you in advance and may be anonymized upon request.

Intellectual Property

All training programs, wellness guides, educational materials, and resources provided by Lavinia Calumby are for your personal use only. They may not be copied, shared, resold, or distributed to any third party without written permission.
