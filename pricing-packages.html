A note on my approach

I am a NASM Certified Personal Trainer and IIN Certified Health Coach. My health coaching focuses on building sustainable habits, improving your relationship with food and your body, managing stress, and creating a lifestyle that supports your long term wellbeing. I do not prescribe diets or provide medical nutrition therapy as this falls within the scope of a registered dietitian. What I offer is something equally powerful: genuine, lasting behavior change rooted in self-awareness, self-compassion, and science-backed wellness principles.
